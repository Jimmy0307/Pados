# Product editions (NON-BINDING draft)

No licensing checks are implemented or planned for the community edition. This is a positioning draft only.

## Community

- Single user; local deployment (LOCAL_SINGLE_USER) and private-LAN nodes (TRUSTED_LAN).
- The full governed chain: context, work package, providers, receipts, validation, CAS, audit, Mission Control, Decision Inbox.
- Basic providers: Codex/Claude CLI workers, and local models via Local Compute Nodes (Ollama, OpenAI-compatible, llama.cpp, deterministic).
- Enterprise generic capability contracts (manifests, classification, retrieval, rerank, lineage, budgets, SecretRef).
- Doctor, CLI, one-command demo.

## Enterprise (potential future)

- SSO and identity federation; centralized RBAC and policy administration.
- Fleet management and hardened node identity (asymmetric / mTLS); HA.
- Compliance packs, advanced audit reporting, centralized observability.
- Enterprise connector packs; managed deployment; support and SLA.
