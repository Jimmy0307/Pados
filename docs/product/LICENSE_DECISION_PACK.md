## Options to decide (non-binding)

- **A. Apache-2.0 core, proprietary enterprise packages.** Maximum adoption, patent grant, clean open-core split. Risk: hosted competitors.
- **B. AGPL-3.0 core plus commercial license (CLA).** Protects against SaaS resale; lowers enterprise adoption.
- **C. MPL-2.0 core.** Middle ground; file-level reciprocity.
- **D. BSL 1.1 with a change date to Apache-2.0.** Protects the commercial phase; not open source until the change date.
