# Contribution model evaluation (DCO vs CLA)

> **Decision recorded (owner decision OD-1..OD-4).** PADOS Core is licensed `AGPL-3.0-only`, with separate commercial licensing available from the copyright holder (dual licensing); separable SDKs/schemas may be `Apache-2.0` only under `sdk/`; contributions will require a CLA. See `LICENSING.md`, `COMMERCIAL.md` and `docs/legal/CLA_RECOMMENDATION.md`. The DCO-only recommendation below predates the dual-licensing decision and is superseded: a DCO alone does not preserve commercial relicensing.

| Criterion | DCO 1.1 (sign-off) | CLA (individual/corporate) |
|---|---|---|
| Contributor friction | Low (`git commit -s`) | Higher (legal agreement, signing service) |
| Copyright | Stays with contributors | Usually license grant, sometimes assignment |
| Fit with Apache-2.0 inbound=outbound | Natural | Not required |
| Relicensing flexibility later | Limited (needs consent of contributors) | Higher |
| Enterprise contributor acceptance | Good | Corporate CLAs are often required by legal departments |

**Recommendation (current).** The dual-licensing decision has been made, so contributions require a CLA (or an equivalent inbound license grant) drafted by counsel; a DCO alone is not sufficient. See `docs/legal/CLA_RECOMMENDATION.md`. *(Historical: before that decision, this page recommended DCO 1.1 without a CLA.)*

**Status.** Documented in `release/public/CONTRIBUTING.md`. GitHub enforcement (DCO app or check) is **not enabled**.
